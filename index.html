Key Findings

Critical insights uncovered through systematic data analysis and cross-referencing.

Size Distribution Crisis

Core sizes (Women's 7.5-9.5, Men's 9.5-11.5) represent 72% of population demand but only receive 45% inventory allocation. This 27-33% gap is driving missed sales opportunities.

Inventory Allocation 45%

Neutral Trainer Success

The Neutral Trainer category shows the best alignment at 91% (Women's) and 96% (Men's), representing 59.8% of total sales. This category should remain the primary focus for inventory investment.

Brand Leadership

Brooks dominates with 25.5% sales share, followed by HOKA (18.3%) and ASICS (19.3%). However, ASICS shows a concerning -7.4% inventory vs sales deviation, indicating significant understocking.

Width Availability Gap

Wide width (D) inventory sits at only 25% for women's core sizes despite representing 62% of the width curve alignment demand. Extra wide widths (2E/4E) are nearly non-existent in stock.

Wide (D): -38% Extra Wide: Critical

Plated Category Overstock

Plated shoes show 5.8x inventory vs ideal scaling for women's, while representing only 2.2% of sales. This category needs immediate reallocation to prevent dead stock accumulation.

Overstock Ratio 5.8x

Replenishment Priority

Brooks Adrenaline, HOKA Bondi, and ASICS Kayano are the top 3 sellers but show critical inventory gaps. Immediate replenishment recommended for sizes 7-9 (Women's) and 9-11 (Men's).
