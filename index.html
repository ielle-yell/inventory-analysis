How It Works

Scoring Methodology

What the Score Means

The distribution score measures how well your inventory aligns with expected demand. A score of 100 means perfect alignment - inventory percentages match demand percentages exactly.

90-100AExcellent alignment
80-89BGood alignment
70-79CNeeds improvement
60-69DPoor alignment
0-59FCritical gaps

Why Core Sizes Matter

Core sizes (Women's 7.5-9.5, Men's 9.5-11.5) represent approximately 72% of total demand. These sizes are weighted 5x in the scoring calculation.

Weight System
Core Sizes: 5x
Higher impact on score
Non-Core: 1x
Lower impact

Order Efficiency

Orders targeting underserved core sizes improve your score. Orders to already-well-stocked sizes (especially non-core) can decrease your score by creating further distribution imbalance. Use the "Auto-Fill Optimal" button to automatically distribute your order budget to the sizes that need it most.
